1. Information We Collect
	1.	User Images
	•	Meal Photos: Estimate portions and track nutrition.
	•	Progress Shots: Monitor body-composition changes.
	2.	Profile & Health Data
	•	Account Info: Name, email, password.
	•	Health Metrics: Age, sex, height, weight, activity level.
	3.	Logs & Entries
	•	Workout Data: Exercises, sets, reps, duration, calories burned.
	•	Nutrition Logs: Manual or barcode-scanned foods with macro/micronutrients.
	4.	Chatbot Messages
	•	Encrypted history for seamless conversations.
	5.	Device & Usage
	•	App-specific identifiers, crash reports, analytics.
	6.	Health Integrations
	•	Optional sync with Apple HealthKit/Google Fit for workouts, steps, heart rate, sleep.
	7.	Subscription Transactions
	•	Anonymized receipts; payment details handled by app stores.

2. How We Use Information
	•	Personalization: Tailor workout plans and nutrition goals.
	•	Analytics: Improve app features and performance.
	•	Communication: Deliver updates, tips, and customer support.

3. Sharing & Disclosure
	•	With Your Consent: Export logs, share progress, connect coaches.
	•	Service Providers: Hosting, analytics, email, payment processors (under strict contracts).
	•	Legal Requirements: Court orders, subpoenas, fraud prevention.
	•	Business Transfers: In mergers or acquisitions, with prior notice.

4. Data Security
	•	Encryption: TLS in transit, AES-256 at rest.
	•	Access Control: Least-privilege principle for staff.
	•	Audits: Regular security assessments.

5. Data Retention
	•	Active Account: Retain logs and chat history.
	•	Financial Records: Retained for compliance.
	•	Deletion: User can delete account; purge within 30 days.

6. Your Rights & Choices
	•	Export Data: JSON/CSV exports via the App.
	•	Edit/Delete: Modify or remove any entries or profile info.
	•	Opt-Out: Disable analytics or health integrations in Settings.
	•	Account Deletion: Full data erasure on request.

7. Children’s Privacy

SyntraFit is for users aged 13+. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. International Transfers

Data may be processed in Cyprus or other jurisdictions. We use EU Standard Contractual Clauses for EEA transfers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We’ll update this document as needed. Check back here for the Last Updated date.
