SyntraFit Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the SyntraFit mobile application (the “App”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects the information you provide when using our fitness and nutrition assistant. By downloading, installing, or using SyntraFit, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

We limit our collection to only what is necessary to deliver and improve core App functionality:
	1.	User-Provided Images
	•	Meal Photos: Users may upload photos of meals or snacks for portion‐size estimation and nutrition analysis.
	•	Progress Photos: Optional before/after workout photos for tracking body‐composition changes.
	•	All images are processed on-device when possible and stored encrypted in our backend solely to provide the requested prediction or analysis.
	2.	Profile & Personal Data
	•	Account Information: Name, email address, username, secure password.
	•	Demographics & Health Metrics: Age, sex, height, weight, activity level—used to calculate calorie targets and workout plans.
	3.	Workout & Nutrition Logs
	•	Exercise Data: Exercise type, sets, reps, weights, duration, calories burned.
	•	Nutrition Data: Manual entries or barcode-scanned foods (including calories, macronutrients, micronutrients).
	4.	Private Chat Messages
	•	Messages sent to our in-App chatbot are stored encrypted, solely to maintain conversation history and improve future responses. They are not used for marketing or shared without consent.
	5.	Device & Usage Data
	•	Device Identifiers: A randomized App-specific identifier (no device-level fingerprinting).
	•	Crash Logs & Performance Metrics: Aggregated to improve stability and performance.
	•	Usage Analytics: Feature usage and screen-flow analytics to help us understand which features are most valuable.
	6.	Third-Party Health Integrations
	•	With your permission, we may integrate with Apple HealthKit or Google Fit to read/write workout summaries, step counts, heart-rate data, sleep analysis, etc.
	•	You can disable any integration at any time in your device’s Settings.
	7.	Subscription & Payment Data
	•	If you subscribe to SyntraFit Premium, we collect & store anonymized transaction receipts via our payment processor (e.g., Apple App Store, Google Play). We do not store full credit card details.

2. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may share information only in these limited scenarios:
	1.	With Your Consent
	•	You explicitly opt in to share data (e.g., exporting your workout log to a coach, sharing progress photos on social media, or connecting with third-party services).
	2.	Service Providers & Analytics
	•	We employ trusted third parties for hosting, analytics (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics), email delivery (e.g., SendGrid), and payment processing (e.g., Stripe). All third parties are bound by contracts to only process data as necessary to deliver their service to us.
	3.	Legal & Safety Exceptions
	•	If required by law (court order, subpoena) or to respond to legal processes, protect our rights or property, or to prevent fraud or imminent harm.
	4.	Corporate Transactions
	•	In the event SyntraFit is acquired, merged, or reorganized, user data may be transferred as part of the transaction. We will notify you before any change of ownership.

3. Data Security
	•	Encryption: All data in transit is protected via TLS. Sensitive data at rest (images, logs, health metrics) is encrypted using industry-standard AES-256.
	•	Access Controls: We follow the principle of least privilege, granting employees and contractors access only to what is necessary.
	•	Regular Audits: We conduct periodic security audits and vulnerability assessments.

4. Data Retention
	•	We retain your workout logs, nutrition entries, and chatbot history for as long as your account is active.
	•	Subscription and transaction records are kept to comply with financial regulations.
	•	You may request deletion of your entire account and all associated data at any time via the Settings screen; deleted data is purged from our servers within 30 days.

5. Your Rights & Choices
	•	Access & Portability: You can export your personal and fitness data in JSON or CSV at any time from within the App.
	•	Correction: Edit or delete any exercise, nutrition entry, or profile field.
	•	Opt-Out: Turn off analytics data collection in Settings. You can also disable HealthKit/Google Fit access.
	•	Account Deletion: Permanently delete your account from the App; all personal data will be erased.

6. Children’s Privacy

SyntraFit is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us to request deletion.

7. International Data Transfers

Your data may be stored and processed in servers located in Cyprus or in other jurisdictions where we or our service providers operate. We ensure appropriate safeguards (e.g., EU Standard Contractual Clauses) are in place for any transfers outside the European Economic Area.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, compliance, or operational reasons. When we post an updated policy, the “Last Updated” date below will be revised. Continued use of SyntraFit after any change indicates your acceptance of the revised policy.
